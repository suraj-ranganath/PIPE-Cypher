## v3 issues found and fixed during the runs
- Template and induction replies were truncated at 2,048 tokens because GLM's reasoning consumed most of the budget, so induction failed silently. Fixed by giving template calls 3x the budget and retrying once at 4x; the first launch was aborted.
- The id-slot filler duplicated id phrases ("with companyId company with id 42") in 160 questions. Fixed at the source, and the export normalizes them (ids unchanged, original wording kept). Downstream reused all unaffected predictions.
- MLX returned Gemma-2's `<end_of_turn>` as text. Prediction cleaning now strips end-of-turn markers, and the stored raw outputs were re-extracted without new model calls.
- 91 gpt-6-luna audit replies were truncated at 2,048 tokens. They were re-audited at 4,096 tokens (1 error remains).
